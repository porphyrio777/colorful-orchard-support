Upcoming advertising release — October 25, 2026

Version 1.0.1 (build 22), prepared for App Store review, enables our production Google AdMob Banner and optional rewarded Shuffle. It requests ATT authorization before ad eligibility; refusing does not block the game and disables publisher personalization and publisher first-party ID. Google may process advertising identifiers, coarse location, interactions and diagnostics as explained in the full advertising privacy policy. The existing ad-free 1.0 (19) and test-ad 1.0.1 (21) policies below remain version-specific.

Advertising-enabled TestFlight builds — October 21, 2026

TestFlight 1.0.1 (21) includes Google AdMob and UMP, a bottom Banner and optional rewarded Shuffle from level 31. This beta uses Google test ads. Google SDKs may process IP address and approximate location, app/device identifiers, advertising interactions, diagnostics and performance data, even though puzzle saves and earned game coins stay on your device. Where required, privacy choices are requested before ads; optional choices can be declined, and available choices can be revisited in Settings. See the full policy for advertising-enabled builds.

The ad-free public App Store release 1.0 (19) remains covered by the policy below.

Colorful Orchard: Fruit Pairs · Ad-free version 1.0 (build 19) · Updated October 13, 2026

简体中文

Gameplay stays on your device

The ad-free app does not require an account. Saved boards, level progress, earned game coins, Undo history, practice completion and sound/haptic preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not upload these records to a server or collect them through a developer analytics service. This version does not include an advertising, third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

No ads, purchases or tracking

Build 19 displays no Banner or rewarded ads and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Game coins are earned through play and cannot be bought with money. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, access advertising identifiers, or send tracking information to an advertising network. Its bundled 300 levels can be played offline, and remote level fetching is disabled in this release.

Permissions and local data

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, photo-library or contacts permissions. You can adjust sound and haptics in the settings screen. Deleting the app removes its local data; restoring a device backup may restore your saved progress. The game is a general-audience puzzle and is not configured for Apple's Kids category.

Support and this website

If you choose to email us, we receive the information you provide and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive personal information. The support/privacy links open this public website; it is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process normal website requests under GitHub's privacy practices. The app does not send your game saves to this site. Apple may process App Store, system diagnostics or aggregate analytics under its own policies and your device choices; the app does not implement a separate developer telemetry service.

Earlier test builds

Some earlier TestFlight/development builds integrated Google AdMob and UMP, including test advertisements. Their advertising data practices are described in the October 12 advertising-build policy. That SDK and its ad requests are absent from the ad-free build 19 covered above. If a future release adds advertising or another data service, this policy and the App Store disclosures will be updated.

Colorful Orchard: Fruit Pairs · 无广告版本 1.0（build 19）· 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 13 日

游戏数据保存在设备上

无广告版本无需账号。棋盘存档、关卡进度、游戏金币、撤销记录、教学完成状态以及声音和触感偏好保存在本机。我们不会将这些数据上传到服务器，也不会通过开发者分析服务收集这些数据。本版本不包含广告、第三方分析或崩溃上报 SDK。

没有广告、内购或跟踪

Build 19 不展示底部 Banner 或激励广告，没有内购或订阅。金币通过游戏获得，不能用现金购买。游戏不请求 App 跟踪透明度权限，不访问广告标识，也不向广告网络发送跟踪信息。内置 300 关可离线玩，本版本关闭远程关卡请求。

权限与本地数据

游戏不申请定位、相机、麦克风、照片或通讯录权限。可在设置中调整声音和触感。删除 App 会移除本地数据；恢复设备备份可能恢复进度。游戏面向大众，未配置 Apple 儿童类别。

支持与网站

主动发邮件时，我们会收到你提供的信息，用于回复问题；请勿发送敏感个人信息。支持和隐私链接打开这个公开网站，由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能依其政策处理常规访问请求。游戏不会把存档发送到本网站。Apple 可能依据自身政策和设备设置处理 App Store 信息、系统诊断或汇总分析；游戏未集成独立的开发者遥测服务。

较早的测试版本

部分较早的 TestFlight 或开发版本集成过 Google AdMob 和 UMP，包括测试广告；相关数据处理见10 月 12 日广告测试版本政策。本政策所述的无广告 build 19 已移除该 SDK 和广告请求。未来如加入广告或其他数据服务，会同步更新政策及 App Store 隐私申报。
