简体中文

Local gameplay data

The app does not require an account. Your saved boards, level progress, earned coins, Undo history, onboarding completion and sound/haptic preferences are stored on your device. We do not upload these saved gameplay records to a player-account server. The current production configuration does not send custom gameplay analytics to the developer.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). It may display a bottom Banner and optional full-screen rewarded ads. From level 31, you may choose an ad for a free Shuffle; the first 30 levels provide free help without a rewarded ad. Ad availability depends on network access, your privacy choices and provider approval; some test builds show demo ads.

Google's advertising SDK can process your IP address (including approximate location derived from it), app- or developer-scoped device identifiers, advertising impressions and interactions, crash/diagnostic information and performance data. Google uses this data for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security and improving its services, as described in its own policies. This means an ads-enabled app is not a “data not collected” app, even though saved puzzles stay on the device.

Where required, the app obtains privacy choices through Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can revisit available advertising privacy choices in the game's sound/settings screen when the provider requires an entry point. Declining optional choices does not prevent you from playing. Rewarded ads are initiated by your choice; closing one before a reward is earned does not spend coins or grant a Shuffle.

Tracking and permissions

The current app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Under iOS permission rules, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is unavailable without tracking authorization. It does not request location, camera, microphone, photo-library or contacts permissions. Other identifiers and advertising-related information may still be processed by Google, subject to your device settings and privacy choices. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob policies.

Purchases, network access and children

The current app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. The included puzzles can be played offline; ads and consent updates require internet access. The app is a general-audience puzzle and is not currently configured for Apple's Kids category. It does not ask players for their name, email, birth date or age. Please contact us with questions about a child's privacy.

Removing data and contacting us

Deleting the app removes its local data from the device; restoring a device backup may restore saved app data. This does not automatically delete information already handled by Google. Use Google's privacy controls for information held by Google. For app support or privacy questions, contact team.emitu@gmail.com; please do not send sensitive personal information.

We update this policy when the app's data handling changes. These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process normal website request information when you visit.

隐私政策

保存在设备上的数据

游戏无需账号。棋盘存档、关卡进度、游戏金币、撤销记录、新手教学完成状态以及声音和触感偏好保存在设备上。我们不会把这些存档上传到玩家账号服务器。当前正式配置不会向开发者发送自定义游戏分析事件。

广告与隐私选择

游戏集成 Google AdMob 和 UMP 同意管理，可能显示底部 Banner 和玩家主动选择的全屏激励广告。第 31 关起，可选择看广告免费打乱一次；前 30 关直接提供免费帮助。广告是否可用取决于网络、隐私选择和平台审核；部分测试版本显示测试广告。

Google 广告 SDK 可能处理 IP 地址及由此推算的大致位置、App 或开发者范围内的设备标识、广告展示和互动、崩溃与诊断信息、性能数据。Google 按其政策将这些信息用于广告展示、效果衡量、分析、安全及服务改进。因此，包含广告的版本不能被描述为“完全不收集数据”，即使棋盘存档保存在本机。

需要时，游戏在请求广告前通过 Google 表单取得隐私选择。当平台要求隐私设置入口时，可在游戏声音/设置页面重新管理广告隐私选择。拒绝可选的隐私选项不影响继续玩游戏。激励广告由玩家主动发起；尚未获得广告奖励就关闭，不扣金币，也不发放打乱。

跟踪与权限

当前 App 不请求 App 跟踪透明度权限；未获跟踪授权时，iOS 不提供可用的 IDFA 广告标识。不申请定位、相机、麦克风、照片或通讯录权限。Google 仍可能根据设备设置与隐私选择处理其他设备标识和广告相关信息。详情请参阅 Google 隐私政策及 AdMob 政策。

购买、联网与儿童

当前版本没有内购或订阅。内置关卡可离线玩，广告和同意信息更新需要联网。游戏面向大众，当前未配置 Apple 儿童类别。不会询问玩家姓名、邮箱、生日或年龄。如有儿童隐私问题，请联系我们。

删除数据和联系

删除 App 会移除设备上的本地数据；恢复设备备份可能恢复 App 数据。这不会自动删除 Google 已处理的信息。请通过 Google 隐私工具管理其持有的信息。游戏或隐私问题请联系 team.emitu@gmail.com，请避免提供敏感个人信息。数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策。本支持网站托管于 GitHub Pages，访问页面时 GitHub 可能处理常规网站请求信息。
