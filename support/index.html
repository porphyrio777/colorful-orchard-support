Support

For Colorful Orchard: Fruit Pairs, email team.emitu@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and affected level; do not send sensitive personal information.

Playing and saving

Tap a fruit to highlight matching types. Slide a fruit in a straight line toward its match, or into a space that aligns the pair. Boards, progress and coins save locally. Use the home screen's “How to play” to replay the practice course.

Help and coins

The ad-free build 18 has no advertising, purchases or subscriptions. Hint and Shuffle are free for the first 30 levels. Later, a Hint costs 5 earned game coins and a Shuffle costs 15. Undo and restarting a level are free; if your balance is low, try a different move order or earn coins by completing levels.

Sound and privacy

Use the speaker/settings button to change music, effects and haptics. See the privacy policy for details.
