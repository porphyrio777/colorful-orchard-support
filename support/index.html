Support

For Colorful Orchard: Fruit Pairs, email team.emitu@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and affected level; do not send sensitive personal information.

Playing and saving

Tap a fruit to highlight matching types. Slide a fruit in a straight line toward its match, or into a space that aligns the pair. Boards, progress and coins save locally. Use the home screen's “How to play” to replay the practice course.

Shuffle and ads

Hint and Shuffle are free for the first 30 levels. Later, Shuffle offers 15 coins or a rewarded ad. One successful help ad is available per active level attempt, shared with Hint. Loading failure or an exit without an earned reward does not charge coins or use that quota. Ads may be unavailable while the provider reviews the app or when offline.

Sound and privacy

Use the speaker/settings button to change music, effects, haptics and any available advertising privacy choices. See the privacy policy for details.
