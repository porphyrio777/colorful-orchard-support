Colorful Orchard: Fruit Pairs

A little fruit-pair puzzle for iPhone. Tap to find matching fruit, then slide in a straight line to clear pairs.

The ad-free version includes 300 offline levels, with no ads, in-app purchases or account. This site provides support and privacy information for the app. English and Simplified Chinese are supported.
